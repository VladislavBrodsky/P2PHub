# Pintopay Blog Writing Guidelines

To maintain the high quality and "viral" nature of the Pintopay blog, all new articles must adhere to the following guidelines. Our goal is not just to inform, but to **persuade, inspire, and mobilize**.

## 1. The Core Philosophy
We do not write "SEO filler". We write **Manifestos**. Every article should feel like a secret piece of knowledge being shared with an inner circle.
*   **Target Audience:** The "Sovereign Individual" — someone who is tired of the traditional system and hungry for freedom.
*   **Tone:** Authoritative, Urgent, Direct, and Empowering.
*   **Voice:** "Top 1%" insider. We are not asking them to join; we are inviting them to evolve.

## 2. Structure & Formatting
Viral content is easy to read. Huge walls of text are forbidden.
*   **Length:** 2000 - 3000 characters (approx. 400-600 words).
*   **Headings:** Use H1 for the title (Provocative) and H3 for subsections.
*   **Paragraphs:** Keep them short (1-3 sentences max).
*   **Formatting:** Use **Bold** for emphasis, bullet points for lists, and short punchy sentences.

## 3. The "Hook" (First Paragraph)
You have 3 seconds to grab attention.
*   **Do:** Start with a controversial statement, a surprising fact, or a direct question.
    *   *Example:* "The bank branch is the Blockbuster Video of finance."
    *   *Example:* "Let's strip away the noise. You're not here for another side hustle."
*   **Don't:** Start with "In this article, we will discuss..." or generic introductions.

## 4. The "Value" (Body)
 deliver specific, actionable, or "mind-shifting" value.
*   **The Problem:** Clearly articulate the pain point (e.g., slow banks, inflation, lack of opportunity).
*   **The Solution:** Position Pintopay/Crypto not just as a tool, but as the *only logical evolution*.
*   **The Pivot:** Move from the general problem to the specific Pintopay advantage (Velocity, Sovereignty, Global Access).

## 5. The "Close" (Call to Action)
End with a bang. Leave the reader ready to run through a wall.
*   **Directive:** Tell them exactly what to do or how to think.
*   **Empowerment:** Remind them that the power is in their hands.
*   **Signature:** End with a bold, short phrase.
    *   *Example:* "Build the engine. Own your time."
    *   *Example:* "Tap. Pay. Gone."

## 6. Key Themes & Vocabulary
Use these concepts to reinforce the brand identity:
*   **Velocity:** Speed of money, $1/minute, instant settlement.
*   **Sovereignty:** Own your keys, be your own bank, freedom from control.
*   **Legacy vs. Future:** Banks = Dinosaurs/Primitive. Crypto = Evolution/Future.
*   **Network Effects:** Your network is your net worth, scaling, leverage.
*   **Global/Borderless:** No geography, world citizen, travel light.

## 7. Language Specifics
*   **English:** Use punchy, modern business/crypto slang (but explain if niche). Avoid academic or overly formal language.
*   **Russian:** Maintain the "expert" tone. Use terms like "Суверенитет" (Sovereignty), "Масштабирование" (Scaling), "Ликвидность" (Liquidity). Avoid dry, bureaucratic russian.

## 8. Quality Control Checklist
Before publishing, ask:
1.  Does the title make me want to click?
2.  Is the first sentence impossible to ignore?
3.  Does it make the reader feel smarter or more powerful?
4.  Is it visually scannable?
5.  Does it sound like a human expert, not a corporate bot?

---
**Remember:** We are not selling a product. We are selling a **new economic reality**. Write accordingly.
